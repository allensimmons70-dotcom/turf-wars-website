Frequently Asked Questions

These quick fixes solve many common issues.

Why can’t Turf Wars access my microphone?

Open your device Settings, find Turf Wars, and make sure microphone permission is enabled. Then close and reopen the app.

Why is my recording silent?

Confirm microphone permission is enabled, check that your device microphone is not blocked, disconnect Bluetooth audio devices temporarily, and record another short test take.

Why can’t I hear my take during playback?

Increase your device volume and confirm silent mode or another connected audio device is not affecting playback. Close and reopen the app if the issue continues.

Are my recordings uploaded publicly?

Turf Wars does not automatically publish your recordings. A recording remains part of your app experience unless you intentionally use an available sharing option.

How do I delete a saved take?

Open your saved takes inside the Flow Lab, select the take you want to remove, and use the available delete option.

How do I change my MC name or profile?

Open your Turf Wars profile and edit the available profile information. Save your changes before leaving the screen.

The app froze or stopped responding. What should I do?

Close Turf Wars completely and reopen it. If the problem continues, restart your device and confirm you are using the latest available version of the app.

How do I report inappropriate content or a safety concern?

Email Turf Wars Support with a clear description of the concern and any relevant details. Do not include sensitive personal information that is not necessary to investigate the report.

Reporting a bug

To help us investigate quickly, include:

  • Your device model
  • Your operating system version
  • Your Turf Wars app version
  • A description of what happened
  • The steps that caused the problem
  • A screenshot, when helpful
