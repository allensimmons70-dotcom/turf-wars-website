PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Turf Wars (“Turf Wars,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) handles information when you use Turf Wars: Flow Labs, our website, and related services.

By using Turf Wars, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide information such as:

  • An MC name or profile information
  • Support messages and bug reports
  • Information included when contacting us
  • Audio recordings created through the app

3. Audio Recordings

Turf Wars uses microphone access to allow you to record performances and practice takes.

Turf Wars does not automatically publish your recordings. Recordings remain part of your app experience unless you intentionally use an available sharing feature.

Device permissions can be managed through your device settings.

4. Automatically Collected Information

We may receive limited technical information necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the service. This may include:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash or diagnostic information
  • General feature-performance information

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain Turf Wars
  • Enable app features
  • Respond to support requests
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Improve performance and user experience
  • Protect the safety and security of the service
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers that assist with operating, hosting, maintaining, analyzing, or securing Turf Wars. These providers may only use information as necessary to perform services for us.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children’s Privacy

Turf Wars is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and address the matter.

10. Your Choices

You may control microphone and other device permissions through your device settings.

You may also contact us to ask questions about information associated with a support request or to request deletion when applicable.

11. Third-Party Services and Links

Turf Wars may contain links to third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of those third parties.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Turf Wars develops. When changes are made, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

13. Contact Us

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy can be submitted through the Turf Wars Support Center or by email at support@turfwars.app .
