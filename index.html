Meet the panel

Five judges. Five personalities.

Every judge brings a different attitude to the booth. Some will encourage you. Some will challenge you. None of them are easy to forget.

THE ENFORCER

Bishop Steel

Strict, direct, and hard to impress. Bishop Steel respects control, confidence, and bars that land with purpose.

THE TECHNICIAN

Lil Binary

Focused on timing, precision, and structure. Lil Binary listens for the little details that separate a decent take from a sharp one.

THE SOUL

Saint Mercy

Emotion matters. Saint Mercy pays attention to feeling, presence, and whether the performance actually connects.

THE VETERAN

Uncle Snaps

Old-school ears and no patience for lazy delivery. Uncle Snaps values rhythm, confidence, and respect for the craft.

THE MYSTERY

Velvet Ghost

Quiet, unpredictable, and hard to read. Velvet Ghost listens for originality, atmosphere, and the kind of delivery that sticks with you.
